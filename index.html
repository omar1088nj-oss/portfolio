Fruit Hub — E-Commerce Mobile Application

Modern Cross-Platform E-Commerce Experience

Project Overview

Fruit Hub is a modern cross-platform e-commerce mobile application designed for ordering fresh fruits and vegetables. It delivers a seamless, intuitive, and secure shopping experience, allowing users to browse, manage, and purchase organic produce effortlessly.

Key Features & Functionalities

Dynamic Product Catalog

Browse a wide variety of fresh items with detailed information, including pricing, measurement units, and high-quality imagery.

Smart Search & Sorting

Filter and sort products by price, alphabetical order, or best sellers.

Cart Management

Adjust quantities dynamically, calculate totals in real time, and remove items smoothly.

Multi-Step Checkout Flow

Complete a structured three-step checkout: payment selection, validated shipping address, and order summary review.
